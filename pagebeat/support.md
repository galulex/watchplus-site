# Pagebeat — Support

Something not working, or a question? Write to [watch@ab.team](mailto:watch@ab.team) with your
watch model, its watchOS version and what happened. We answer within a couple of days.

## How a session works

- **Set your book.** Tap the book on the home screen: its title and pages, and how far you are.
  The cover is found for you.
- **Start.** Tap **START READING** when you open your book. The first time, allow Pagebeat to read
  your heart rate.
- **Read.** Lower your wrist as you like: raise it and Pagebeat is still there. **Pause** if you
  stop for a while.
- **Finish.** Tap **Finish**, turn the Digital Crown to the pages you read, and **Save**. You see
  the pages, the time, and how your heart rate changed.

## Questions

**The heart rate says "No reading".**
The watch had no heart rate for 30 seconds. Wear it snug, a finger's width above the wrist bone.
Check that Pagebeat may read heart rate: on the iPhone, Health → Sharing → Apps → Pagebeat. The
session goes on either way.

**Why does a workout icon show while I read?**
A live heart rate on Apple Watch needs a workout session, so a reading session runs as one (Mind
and Body). It also keeps Pagebeat on screen when you raise your wrist. When you finish it is
thrown away: no workout is saved to Health or Fitness.

**The reminder doesn't come.**
It is skipped on days you have already met your goal. Otherwise check that it is on (the bell on
the home screen) and that notifications for Pagebeat are allowed in the Watch app on your iPhone.

**My book has no cover, or the wrong one.**
Covers come from Open Library, so the watch needs a connection, and the book has to be in their
catalogue. Check the title's spelling; until a cover is found, the book shows its initials.

**How do I start over?**
Delete Pagebeat and install it again: everything it keeps is on the watch, so this clears it all.

**How do the free trial and Pro work?**
Pagebeat is free to try for 7 days: tap **START READING** and then **Start 7-day free trial**.
After 7 days, reading sessions, heart rate, page logging and reminders need Pagebeat Pro, a
one-time purchase (no subscription). Your books, progress and streak stay either way. The trial
is tied to your Apple Account, so reinstalling doesn't restart it.

**I bought Pro but it's locked.**
Open the Pro screen (GET PRO on the home screen) and tap **Restore**, signed in with the Apple
Account you bought it with. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

**Is it a medical device?**
No. The heart rate is for your interest only and does not diagnose anything.
