# Pagebeat — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

Pagebeat ("the app") is a reading tracker for Apple Watch by Active Bridge, LLC. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## The short version

Everything Pagebeat knows stays on your Apple Watch. There are no servers of ours, no accounts,
no analytics and no ads, and we receive nothing. The app goes online for one thing only: finding
your book's cover on Open Library (see below).

## Your heart rate

- While a reading session runs, Pagebeat reads your heart rate through Apple Health, only with
  your permission. It shows it live, and at the end how it changed from the start of the session.
- To get a live heart rate, the session runs as a Mind and Body workout on the watch. When you
  finish, that workout is **discarded: Pagebeat saves no workout** to Apple Health or the Fitness
  app. The heart-rate readings the watch takes meanwhile are kept by Apple Health as all your
  heart-rate readings are, under Apple's own privacy policy.
- The heart rate a session ended on is kept on the watch, for the Pagebeat complication. Nothing
  else from Health is kept.
- Heart-rate data is never sent anywhere, never stored in iCloud, and never used for advertising.

## Your books and reading

- Your books, their page counts and progress, the pages you read each day, your goal, your streak
  and your reminder time are stored on the watch only.
- The daily reminder is a notification the watch schedules for itself. Nothing is sent to us to
  deliver it.

## Book covers from Open Library

To find a book's cover, the app sends the book's **title and author**, as you typed them, to
[Open Library](https://openlibrary.org) (run by the non-profit Internet Archive), and downloads
the cover image from it. Nothing else goes with it: no name, no account, no heart rate. Open
Library, like any website, sees the request's IP address; its own
[terms and privacy policy](https://archive.org/about/terms) apply to that. The cover is then kept
on the watch.

## What the app shares

Nothing with us, and nothing with anyone else beyond the Open Library lookup above. We do not
sell, share or use any data for advertising, and there is no tracking across apps or websites.

## Deleting your data, and stopping

- Delete Pagebeat from your watch, and everything it stored goes with it.
- Turn off Pagebeat's access to heart rate at any time on your iPhone: Health → Sharing → Apps →
  Pagebeat. Reading sessions still work, without a heart rate.
- Turn off the reminder in Pagebeat, or its notifications in the Watch app on your iPhone.

## Not a medical device

Pagebeat's heart rate is for your interest only. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any
condition.

## Children

Pagebeat collects nothing that identifies anyone, of any age.

## Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes; the date above shows the current version.
