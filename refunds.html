Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

14 days, no questions asked

If you are not happy with a license — Watch+ or single-app Pro — email watch@ab.team within 14 days of purchase with your order id or the email you paid with. We refund in full to the original payment method. The license is revoked when the refund is issued.

Watch+ renewals

The same 14 days apply to each yearly renewal. You can cancel the membership at any time from the receipt email or through support; Pro stays active until the end of the paid year and nothing is charged after that.

After 14 days

We still want to hear from you. If an app does not work on your watch model, or a Pro feature is broken and we cannot fix it, we will refund regardless of the date.

How long it takes

Refunds are issued by our merchant of record (Creem.io or Lemon Squeezy) within two business days of your request; your bank may take a further 5–10 days to show it.
