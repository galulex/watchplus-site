Turn two Amazfit watches into walkie-talkies. Hold the button, talk, let go: the clip plays on your friend's wrist with a buzz. No accounts.

- Pair with a 6-digit code, on the watch or in the Zepp settings page
- Free: rooms of 2, clips up to 5 s
- **Pro:** rooms of 3+, 10 s clips, enhanced audio, replay

Voice clips pass through our relay and are deleted within two minutes. It's a walkie-talkie, not a secure messenger.
