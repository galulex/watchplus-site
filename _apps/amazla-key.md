Amazla Key turns your watch into a Bluetooth digital key for your Tesla. No phone needed.

- Lock and unlock from the watch
- Passive entry: the car senses your watch nearby
- Walk-up unlock and walk-away lock
- End-to-end encrypted Bluetooth LE, using Tesla's phone-key protocol
- Works standalone; your keys never leave your devices

Pair once on the watch, then it talks to your car directly.
