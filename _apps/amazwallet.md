Loyalty and membership cards on your watch as real, scannable codes. No phone needed at the till.

- Add cards in the Zepp app: name, number, code type, colour. They sync automatically.
- EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, CODE 39, CODE 128 and QR, with check digits validated as you type
- Tap a card to fill the screen at full brightness so scanners read it first time
- Watch face widget and a card page one swipe away

**Pro:** more than 5 cards.
