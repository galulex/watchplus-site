A compass that holds north while your wrist moves, over a world map that marks where you stand.

- The four nearest capitals and regional seats, each with distance and true bearing
- Tap the ring for names, the centre for the readout, the pin for the map
- Runs entirely on the watch: the map and 2,659 cities live on it. No phone, no signal.
- A tilt-controlled mini-game walks you through calibration
