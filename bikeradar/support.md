# Bike Radar — Support

Something not working, another radar, or a question? Write to [watch@ab.team](mailto:watch@ab.team)
with your watch model, your radar model and what happened. We answer within a couple of days.

## How a ride works

- **Turn the radar on.** A Garmin Varia or a Varia-compatible rear radar.
- **Open Bike Radar on the watch.** It finds the radar and remembers it. No phone needed on the ride.
- **Ride with the app open.** The screen goes dark between glances; raise your wrist, tap or press
  a button to look. A close car lights it up by itself.

## The screen

- **The number:** metres to the nearest car, white when far, then amber, then red.
- **The ring:** fills as the car closes in. Green: clear road. Grey: no radar.
- **The dots:** one per car behind you, up to 6.

## The buzzes

- **Two short:** a car after a clear road.
- **One short:** one more car.
- **Four strong:** a car within 30 m.
- **One long:** radar lost, again every 30 s until it is back.

## Questions

**It stays on "Looking for radar…".**
Check that the radar is on and not connected to another device: a bike computer or a phone app
can hold its Bluetooth link. Turn the radar off and on, then reopen Bike Radar.

**It connected to a friend's radar.**
Bike Radar remembers the first radar it connects to and prefers it. If yours is off for about
30 seconds, it takes any radar it hears, so turn yours on before you open the app.

**Which radars work?**
Garmin Varia and rear radars with the same Bluetooth profile. Another one? Write to us with its
model and we'll look at adding it.

**Where are the settings?**
On the watch: swipe up from the ride screen. Alerts, screen, car dots, your speed, units (km/h and metres or mph and feet) and logs.

**How do the free ride and Pro work?**
One ride a day is free, with every feature. It counts from the day's first link to the radar, and
a ride that started is never cut short. Bike Radar Pro, a one-time purchase (no subscription),
gives you every ride of the day. Buy it from the app's settings page in the Zepp app on your phone.

**I bought Pro but it's locked.**
Open the app's settings page in the Zepp app, tap **Unlock Pro** and paste the licence key from
your receipt email. Still locked? Write to us with the email you bought it with.

**Is it a safety device?**
No. It is an aid: keep looking and listening behind you.
