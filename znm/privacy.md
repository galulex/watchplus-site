# ZNM — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

ZNM ("the app") is a notification helper for Amazfit / Zepp OS watches. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## The short version

ZNM collects nothing, stores no message content, and sends nothing anywhere. Everything
happens on your phone. The app has **no internet access at all** — it does not declare the
internet permission, so it cannot transmit data even in principle.

## What the app accesses

To do its one job — marking a chat as read when you delete its notification on your
watch — ZNM uses Android's **Notification access**. While that access is granted, Android
lets the app see notifications and when they are removed.

ZNM uses this only to:

- detect that a notification was removed by your watch, and
- trigger that notification's own "mark as read" action.

ZNM does **not** read, copy, log, upload, or retain the contents of your notifications
(message text, sender, images, etc.).

## What the app stores

On your device only, in the app's private storage, ZNM keeps:

- which apps you have switched off,
- a short rolling on-screen activity log (app name, event type and time — never message
  text), capped at the most recent entries.

This data never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its
data.

## What the app shares

Nothing. There are no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no servers.

## Children

ZNM is a utility with no content directed at children and collects no personal data.
