Pro apps for your Amazfit watch.

We build apps for Amazfit smartwatches (Zepp OS). Every app is free to install from the Zepp App Store. A license unlocks the Pro features — in one app, or in all of them with Watch+.

The apps

Amazla Key

Your Tesla phone key on the wrist: unlock, lock, open the trunk, precondition — over Bluetooth, no phone needed.

Authenticator

Two-factor codes (TOTP) on the watch. Scan a QR from the phone, read the code from the wrist. Pro: unlimited accounts and export.

AmazWallet

Loyalty cards, tickets and barcodes on the watch — scannable at the till. Pro: unlimited cards.

Compass+

Compass with bearing to the nearest cities and saved places.

Powerwall

Tesla Powerwall and solar at a glance: battery, grid, home and solar flows.

Watchfaces

TeslaFace and Orbit watchfaces for round and square Amazfit models.

Install: open the Zepp app on your phone → Profile → App Store → search the app name. Works on Amazfit Active, Active 2, Balance, Bip 5/6, Cheetah, GTR 4, GTS 4, T-Rex 3 and other Zepp OS watches.

How it works

What do I get after paying?

A license key. If you bought from the app's settings page it is entered for you; otherwise paste it there once. The watch unlocks within a few seconds.


Which watch do I need?

Any Amazfit running Zepp OS 2 or later. Some apps need hardware the watch must have (Bluetooth LE for Amazla Key, a microphone for voice apps) — each app's store listing says so.
