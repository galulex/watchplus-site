Questions

Good to know.

Which watch do I need?

Any Amazfit on Zepp OS, or a recent Garmin for our Garmin apps. Each app page lists the exact models. Some apps need hardware: Bluetooth for Amazla Key, a microphone and speaker for voice apps.

How do I install an app?

Tap Install on the app’s page on your phone, or open the Zepp app and go to Profile → App Store, then search for the name. For Garmin, use the Connect IQ store.

What do I get after paying?

A license key. If you bought from the app’s settings page, it’s entered for you. If not, paste it there once. The watch unlocks within seconds.

I got a new watch. Do I pay again?

No. One purchase covers up to 3 watches. Enter the same key on the new one.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

Who makes these apps?

Active Bridge, LLC, a small software company in Arizona. Questions or ideas: watch@ab.team. We answer within two business days.
