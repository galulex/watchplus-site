# Strike & Cast — Support

Something not working, or a question? Write to [watch@ab.team](mailto:watch@ab.team) with your
watch model, its watchOS version and what happened. We answer within a couple of days.

## How a session works

- **Start.** Pick your style in the settings, a swipe up from START (spinning, feeder or fly),
  and tap **START**. The
  first time, allow the Fishing workout in Health.
- **Cast.** Fish as you always do: the watch counts each cast from your wrist. Your wrist can stay
  down.
- **Strike.** Tap **CATCH** (or double tap with your thumb and finger, on watches that support
  it), then the size, or let it save itself after three seconds.
- **More.** Swipe up for the barometer and the session's actions: −1 / +1 CAST, **END & SAVE**
  and DISCARD. With the feeder profile there is also a log page to mark a bite or a feeding.
- **After.** The summary, the stats, and **ON THE WEB** for the session's page.

## Questions

**A cast wasn't counted, or one was counted that wasn't a cast.**
The watch looks for a backswing, a hard snap forward, and your wrist going still while the lure
flies. Very soft lobs may be missed: add them with **+1 CAST** on the session page. Take back a
false one with **−1 CAST**, or flip your wrist over and back (the undo gesture, in the settings).
Pick the profile that matches your fishing: feeder and fly casts are gentler than spinning.

**Why a workout?**
A session runs as a Fishing workout so the watch keeps counting with your wrist down, and Strike &
Cast is there when you raise it. END & SAVE saves it in Health and Fitness; DISCARD doesn't.

**The barometer says the trend shows later.**
Apple Watch gives apps no pressure history, so the trend starts with your session: give it ten
minutes.

**WEB PAGE says "No connection".**
Sending needs your iPhone nearby or Wi-Fi. Try again from the summary later: the last session
stays there (Home → the line under START).

**How do I open a session's page on my phone?**
After WEB PAGE, tap **SEND TO PHONE** and send the link to yourself in Messages or Mail.

**How do I keep all my sessions in one place?**
Swipe up from START to the settings → **WEB HISTORY** → send the link to your phone and open it within 15 minutes. Sign in
with Google: from then on every session you send lands in your history at
[strikecast.ab.team](https://strikecast.ab.team).

**The map is empty.**
Places need location access: allow it at your first catch, or later on the watch in Settings →
Privacy & Security → Location Services → Strike & Cast.

**How do I delete a session from the website?**
Write to [watch@ab.team](mailto:watch@ab.team) with its link.

**Is everything included?**
Yes: Strike & Cast is a paid app with everything unlocked. No in-app purchases, no subscription.
Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
