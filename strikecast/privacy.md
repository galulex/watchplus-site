# Strike & Cast — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Strike & Cast ("the app") is a fishing log for Apple Watch by Active Bridge, LLC. This policy
explains what the app and its website, [strikecast.ab.team](https://strikecast.ab.team), do and
do not do with your data.

## The short version

Your fishing sessions are recorded and kept on your Apple Watch. Nothing leaves the watch until
you tap **WEB PAGE** on a session's summary: then that one session goes to our website so it gets
its own page. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we sell nothing.

## On the watch

- **Motion.** The app reads the watch's accelerometer during a session to count your casts. The
  motion itself is not stored; only the time and strength of each cast are.
- **Barometer.** The air pressure is read during a session for the pressure trend and kept with
  the session.
- **Location.** At your first catch the app asks for your location (while using the app). If you
  allow it, each catch keeps where it happened, and a position every 15 minutes during a session
  draws your route. Say no and everything else works, without places.
- **Your sessions** (casts, catches with size, pressure and place, bites and feedings, the route)
  and your settings are stored on the watch only.

## Apple Health

A session runs as a Fishing workout so the watch keeps counting with your wrist down. With your
permission, **END & SAVE** saves it as a Fishing workout in Apple Health and the Fitness app, with
the number of casts and fish; **DISCARD** saves nothing. The app reads nothing from Health. Health
data is never sent to us, never stored in iCloud by us, and never used for advertising.

## When you tap WEB PAGE

That session is sent over HTTPS to strikecast.ab.team, run by us on Cloudflare: the times and
strength of the casts, the catches (time, size, pressure, place), the pressure readings, the
route, the bites and feedings, your watch's time zone, and a random ID the app made for your
watch, so your sessions can be grouped. No name, no email, no Apple Account, no health data.

The session gets a page with a long random address. Anyone with the link can open it; it is not
listed or searchable anywhere. The map on the page shows satellite imagery from Esri, loaded by
your browser, which sees your IP address as any website would.

## Your history (optional)

**WEB HISTORY** in the app's settings gives you a link. Opened on your phone, it asks you to sign
in with Google on strikecast.ab.team; then every session your watch sends goes into your history.
From Google we keep your account ID, name, email and profile picture, to show your history only to
you, and a sign-in cookie. The app itself never signs in to anything.

## Sharing, selling, tracking

We do not sell or share any data, and do not use it for advertising. There is no tracking across
apps or websites. Cloudflare hosts the website and stores its data for us.

## Keeping and deleting

- Sessions you did not send stay on the watch: delete the app and they go with it.
- Sent sessions and an account are kept until you ask us to delete them. Write to
  [watch@ab.team](mailto:watch@ab.team) with a session's link (or the email of your account) and
  we delete it, and your account if you want, within 30 days.
- Turn off location for Strike & Cast at any time: on the watch, Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Location Services. Turn off Health access in Health → Sharing → Apps → Strike & Cast.

## Not a medical device

Strike & Cast is a fishing log. It does not measure or tell you anything about your health.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing that identifies anyone unless they sign
in on the website.

## Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes; the date above shows the current version.
